Dalga Takip: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Dalga Takip (the "app") is provided by Murat Uysal (the "developer"). Contact: dalgatakip@gmail.com

Accounts and personal information

The app does not ask you to create an account or register. We do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

Location

Location permission is optional. If you allow it, your approximate location is used only on your phone, to find the nearest beach. We do not send your location to our servers or to Open-Meteo. If you turn on notifications, your last location is stored on your phone so we can alert you about a nearby beach; it is deleted when you turn notifications off. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings (Settings > Location permission in the app opens them).

Directions

When you tap "Get directions", the location of the beach you selected (coordinates and place name) is passed to the maps app on your phone. We do not send your own location; the maps app calculates the route under its own privacy policy.

Data sent to third-party services

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): the coordinates of the beach you select are sent to get wave and weather forecasts. Your IP address technically reaches this service's servers.
  • Photon / Komoot (photon.komoot.io, OpenStreetMap data): the place name you type in the search box is sent.
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org): when you open the map, the map tiles for the area on screen are downloaded from this service. The requested area and your IP address reach its servers; if you move the map to your own location, the requested area shows your surroundings.
  • Google AdMob (only in versions that show ads; ad-free versions, such as the Huawei AppGallery version, contain no advertising software and do not process this data): in the ad-supported version Google processes your advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we ask for your consent with Google's consent form (UMP); you can review or change it in Settings > Ad privacy options. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings. More information: policies.google.com/privacy

Data stored on your phone

The last forecast cache (up to 20 beaches; anything older than 24 hours is deleted), your selected profile, language, beaches and notification preferences. This data stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

All network connections use HTTPS.

Your rights

Because we hold no account or identifying data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side; you can delete the data on your phone by uninstalling the app. If you are in the EEA or the UK you have the rights provided by data-protection law, including access, correction, erasure and objection; contact us at the address below. Users in Turkey: see the KVKK notice (Turkish).

Disclaimer

Dalga Takip is for information only and does not guarantee sea safety.

  • Wave, wind, weather and sea-state information is generated from model forecasts of a third-party service (Open-Meteo). Forecasts can be wrong, incomplete or delayed, and may differ from real conditions because of local factors such as currents, shallows, the shape of the coast and sudden weather changes. Forecasts for distant days are less reliable.
  • The "Looks suitable", "Caution" and "Risky" badges are an aid based on general thresholds. They do not guarantee that swimming, fishing or going out by boat is safe.
  • When deciding, always put your own observation, the beach flags and warnings, and the instructions of lifeguards and the authorities (coast guard, meteorological service) first. Never leave children unattended in the sea.
  • The app is free and provided "as is". To the extent permitted by law, the developer is not liable for any harm arising from decisions made on the basis of information in the app.
  • In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in Turkey; the Turkish Coast Guard is 158).
